How a round settles

          
Every result comes from the public Squeekchain status feed — the same numbers shown on the wheel above.

          
    
            
  1. Lock-in reading. When you confirm, the game records the current block height from the live feed.
    2. 
            
  2. The 60-second window. If a later reading shows the block height has moved up, the block completed inside your window.
    3. 
            
  3. Deadline check. The on-screen countdown runs a plain 60 seconds from the moment you lock in. When it runs out, the round settles on the first feed reading that arrives at or after the deadline — if the height still hasn't moved, the block missed the window. (Readings are judged by when they arrive, not by the feed's own timestamps: those can arrive out of order, and an earlier version that trusted them voided rounds the feed had actually answered.) Only if no reading at all arrives within 45 seconds past the deadline — the feed genuinely down — is the round voided and your bet refunded rather than left hanging.
    4. 
            
  4. Payout. A correct call returns double your bet in seeds. A wrong one feeds your bet to the hamster.
